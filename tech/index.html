How I work

the same five steps, every build
  1. 1
    Interview the people

    Who handles what, what a good answer looks like, where things get stuck.

  2. 2
    Map the workflow

    The steps, the rules, and the exact point where a person has to decide.

  3. 3
    Build with AI help

    AI for the typing; fixed rules wherever an answer must come out the same every time.

  4. 4
    Check it on real cases

    Run it, look at the output with the owner, log every wrong answer, fix, repeat.

  5. 5
    Hand it over

    Docs, a walkthrough, and enough understanding that the owner can change it without me.
