Case 02 · Agoda
Situation

So what
The aim: questions arrive in one place and reach the right person with the context attached.

The problem

Questions reached the team in three different places.

Colleagues brought the team questions by email, Slack and Teams. Some could take several rounds of back-and-forth before anyone could start on them. A first reply could take hours (my estimate).

I worked with my manager, who set the requirements, on one shared channel where colleagues bring the team their questions.

What it is built to take off the team’s plate

Answering the same common questions one asker at a time. The bots answer and look up what they can, so people spend their time on the cases that need a person.

The final design

Now every question starts in one channel and takes one of three lanes.

The first bot reads every message, so nobody has to tag it. It answers common questions from prepared answers, or calls the bot that looks things up, or the bot that opens a ticket for a person.

What I did first

I interviewed each team member about the cases they should get.

I used their answers to design the routing: which teammate to involve, depending on the question and that person’s area.

What I asked each person

  • What is your specialty, and what is your role?
  • Which problems do you handle?
  • Which kinds of cases should reach you?

How I got there

I worked through four versions to reach the final design.

An existing platform, then a custom bot, then, on the internal platform team’s advice to reuse existing bots, one existing bot, and finally three existing bots working together.

The final design combines three strengths: one bot reads every message without a tag, one writes the clearest answers, and one connects to the team’s ticket system.

Three bots, one job each

I chose each bot for the one thing it does best.

All three are existing bots supplied by Agoda. I combined and configured them, and designed the logic for what each one does and when the first bot calls the others.

What a person still decides

When a case needs a person, a teammate takes it from the ticket.

  • How to resolve the case. Team members investigate and resolve it themselves.
  • The next step. The ticket suggests one, with the findings attached; the teammate decides.

Built-in guards

Only one bot opens tickets, and unclear questions are asked back first.

With three bots in one channel, I designed the routing so an unclear question is asked back first, before it goes any further.

Only the ticket bot connects to the team’s ticket system, so every ticket comes from one place. When a case needs a person, a teammate reviews the findings and decides.

What happened

The channel is live, and cases that need a person reach one with a ticket.

Live
