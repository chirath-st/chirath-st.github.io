The 60-second version

What I did
Mapped the process with my manager, turned the team’s market and invoicing rules into automatic checks, and built a review step where she ticks the opportunities worth pursuing before an editable draft note is prepared for the account manager.
Who did what
I designed and built the tool, with AI coding assistants. The commercial ideas and the final calls were my manager’s.
Runs as
Fixed checks and message templates: the same input always gives the same output, as the guidance I was given asked.

01/ 05

Part 1 of 5: The problem

My manager wanted the checks to run on their own, with the results shown for review.

One check looked for hotels and suppliers selling on Agoda but not yet switched on with partner platforms such as Booking.com and Priceline.

Part 2 of 5: What I built

I mapped the process, then built the team’s rules into the checks.

The team’s rules, built into the checksNot every gap is an opportunity.The checks apply the team’s market and invoicing rules first, so only the gaps that pass reach review.

03/ 05

Part 3 of 5: Who it was for

It was built for my manager and the account managers.

My manager, the business owner, reviews the results. For each opportunity she approves, a draft note is prepared for the account manager.

What I didAsked account managers what a useful note must say and what they should do next.

The review screen I built, part by part2 exhibits · about 1 min

What a person still decides

  • Which flagged opportunities to pursue. The checks only flag possible opportunities; she ticks the ones worth pursuing.
  • What the draft note says. It can be edited before it is saved.

04/ 05

Part 4 of 5: How I checked it

My manager and I reviewed the results at least twice a week.

I showed her working versions early, logged the issues we found, and changed course when she said so.

The review loop, step by step1 exhibit · under 1 min
  1. Show. I showed her working versions early.
  2. Check. Are the flagged hotels and suppliers real candidates? Is the table easy to work with?
  3. Log. I logged the issues we found.
  4. Change. I revised the checks or the layout, then ran them again.

05/ 05

Part 5 of 5: What happened

Live

Built to take three kinds of routine work off the team. The judgement stays with a person, on purpose.

I handed it over with a week spent teaching my manager how it works.
