Case 01 · Agoda
Situation

. Handed over with a week of training.
So what
The aim: the team spends its time on the calls worth making, not on finding them.

The problem

The team ran its sales checks by hand.

One check looked for hotels and suppliers selling on Agoda but not yet switched on with partner platforms such as Booking.com and Priceline.

My manager wanted the checks to run on their own, with the results shown for review.

What it is built to take off the team’s plate

Running the checks by hand. The checks find the possible gaps; people decide which ones to pursue.

Who it was for

It was built for my manager and the account managers.

My manager, the business owner, reviews the results. For each opportunity she approves, a draft note is prepared for the account manager, who follows up.

What I did

I mapped the process, then built the team’s rules into the checks.

  1. Mapped the process with the business owner, my manager: find the gap, apply the rules, review, write the note.
  2. Asked account managers what a useful note must say and what they should do next.
  3. Built the team’s market and invoicing rules into the checks, so gaps that are there on purpose are filtered out before anyone reviews them.
  4. Built a review step where my manager ticks the opportunities worth pursuing, before an editable draft note is prepared for the account manager.

The team’s rules, built into the checks

Gaps that are there on purpose are filtered out before anyone reviews them.

Not every gap is an opportunity. The checks apply the team’s market and invoicing rules first, so only the gaps that pass reach review.

What a person still decides

My manager decides which opportunities are worth pursuing.

  • Which flagged opportunities to pursue. The checks only flag possible opportunities; she ticks the ones worth pursuing.
  • What the draft note says. It can be edited before it is saved.

How I checked it

My manager and I reviewed the results at least twice a week.

I showed her working versions early, logged the issues we found, and changed course when she said so.

What happened

The checks are live, and a person approves every opportunity.

A person still ticks each opportunity before a draft note is prepared for the account manager. I handed it over with a week spent teaching my manager how it works.

So whatThe aim: the team spends its time on the calls worth making, not on finding them.
